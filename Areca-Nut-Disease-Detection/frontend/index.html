Complete guide for successful arecanut farming - from planting to harvest

🌾 1. About Arecanut

Scientific Name: Areca catechu L.
Family: Arecaceae (Palm family)
Common Names: Betel nut, Supari
Economic Use: Chewing, medicinal, industrial

🌡️ 2. Climate & Soil Requirement

Factor Recommendation
Climate Warm & humid; rainfall 750–4500 mm/year
Temperature Ideal 14°C to 36°C
Soil Type Deep, well-drained loamy soil rich in organic matter
pH Range 5.5 – 6.5
Shade Requirement Partial shade during initial years helps early growth

🌱 3. Planting Details

Parameter Details
Best Time for Planting May–June (onset of monsoon)
Plant Spacing 2.7 m × 2.7 m (approx. 1350 plants/ha)
Pit Size 60 cm × 60 cm × 60 cm
Pit Filling Topsoil mixed with 10–15 kg compost & 100 g rock phosphate
Seedlings Age 12–18 months old healthy seedlings
Transplanting Transplant at beginning of rainy season to ensure survival

💧 4. Irrigation Schedule

Season Frequency Remarks
Rainy (Jun–Sep) No irrigation unless dry spell Natural rainfall sufficient
Post-Monsoon (Oct–Feb) Every 7–10 days Maintain soil moisture
Summer (Mar–May) Every 3–4 days Critical period for yield
Method Basin irrigation or drip system preferred – Avoid water stagnation
💡 Tip: Drip irrigation saves up to 40–50% water and improves yield.

🌿 5. Fertilizer & Manure Application

Annual Dose per Palm (after 3 years of planting):

Fertilizer Quantity (g/palm/year) Split & Application Time
Urea (N) 100 g 1/3 in May, 1/3 in September, 1/3 in January
Super Phosphate (P) 40 g Once in May
Muriate of Potash (K) 140 g 2 splits – May & September
Organic Manure 10–15 kg Once in May–June (before monsoon)
🧪 Apply fertilizers in a circular basin (0.75 m radius) around palm, mix with soil, and irrigate immediately.

🌻 6. Intercultural Operations

Operation Description
Weeding Twice a year (May–June and September–October)
Mulching Use coconut husk or dry leaves to retain soil moisture
Soil Mulching In summer, mulch with green leaves or compost
Earthing Up Do after heavy rains to support roots

🐛 7. Pest and Disease Management

Problem Symptoms Control
Yellow Leaf Disease Yellowing of leaves, stunted growth Improve drainage, apply Mancozeb 0.25%
Koleroga (Fruit Rot) Nuts drop prematurely Spray Bordeaux mixture 1% before monsoon
Inflorescence Dieback Blackening of flower stalk Spray Carbendazim 0.1%
Bud Rot Crown rotting, foul smell Cut affected part, spray Copper oxychloride 0.25%
Root Grubs Yellow leaves, roots eaten Apply Chlorpyrifos 0.04% drenching

🌾 8. Flowering and Fruiting Period

Stage Period
Flowering January – March
Fruit Development April – August
Nut Maturity September – December
Harvest Season November – February

🧺 9. Harvesting & Post-Harvest

Step Description
Harvest Time 12–14 months after flowering
Indication Nuts turn orange/red when ripe
Processing Dehusk → Boil nuts → Sun-dry for 40–45 days
Yield 900–1200 kg dry nuts per hectare annually

📋 Treatment Guide

Common Arecanut Diseases and Treatments

🍂 Yellow Leaf Disease (YLD)

Symptoms:

Yellowing of leaves starting from tips, stunted growth, premature nut fall

Treatment:

  • Remove and destroy infected plants
  • Apply root feeding with Tetracycline (0.5g per plant)
  • Maintain field sanitation
  • Use disease-free planting material
Severity: High

🦠 Koleroga (Mahali/Fruit Rot)

Symptoms:

Water-soaked lesions on nuts, white fungal growth, premature nut drop

Treatment:

  • Spray Bordeaux mixture (1%) before monsoon
  • Use Copper Oxychloride (0.25%)
  • Improve drainage in field
  • Remove infected nuts immediately
Severity: High

🍃 Bud Rot

Symptoms:

Rotting of the spindle, drooping of leaves, foul smell from growing point

Treatment:

  • Remove infected spindle and apply Bordeaux paste
  • Spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (0.25%)
  • Ensure proper drainage
  • Avoid overhead irrigation
Severity: High

🐛 Spindle Bug

Symptoms:

Deformed young leaves, stunted growth, yellowing at spindle region

Treatment:

  • Spray Quinalphos (0.05%) or Monocrotophos
  • Apply insecticide during early morning
  • Remove alternate host plants nearby
  • Regular monitoring of pest population
Severity: Medium

🪲 Stem Bleeding

Symptoms:

Dark brown patches on stem, oozing of reddish-brown liquid

Treatment:

  • Scrape affected area and apply Bordeaux paste
  • Root feeding with Tridemorph (2ml per liter)
  • Improve soil drainage
  • Avoid injury to stem during cultivation
Severity: Medium

🌿 Leaf Spot

Symptoms:

Circular brown spots on leaves, leaf yellowing and premature fall

Treatment:

  • Spray Mancozeb (0.25%) or Copper Oxychloride
  • Remove and destroy infected leaves
  • Maintain proper plant spacing
  • Apply balanced fertilization
Severity: Low

🛡️ General Prevention Tips

  • Use disease-resistant varieties when available
  • Maintain proper field sanitation and hygiene
  • Ensure adequate drainage in the plantation
  • Follow recommended fertilization schedule
  • Regular monitoring and early detection of diseases
  • Avoid waterlogging during monsoon season
  • Practice crop rotation where possible
